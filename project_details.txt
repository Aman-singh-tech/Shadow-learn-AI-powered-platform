📌 ShadowLearn – Summary

ShadowLearn is an AI-powered knowledge transfer platform designed to help organizations capture, organize, and preserve critical employee knowledge. It allows teams to record workflows, document solutions, and create task-based learning modules so that expertise is not lost when employees leave or change roles.

The platform uses an intelligent knowledge base to make information easily searchable and accessible, enabling faster onboarding, reducing repeated problem-solving, and minimizing productivity loss. By transforming individual knowledge into a shared organizational asset, ShadowLearn ensures continuity, efficiency, and long-term growth.
